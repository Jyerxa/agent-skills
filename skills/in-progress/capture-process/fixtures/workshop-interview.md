## Supplied answers

### A1 — Scope

We are describing how our small workshop operates today, from a bike being
dropped off to the customer picking it up. Reception opens a job ticket at
drop-off. The same job number stays with the job until collection, even if
we revise the description. Each job is for one bike. A customer can have
several jobs. I only want this branch covered, not the other shop.

### A2 — Preparing a quote

After the ticket is opened, the mechanic inspects the bike and checks whether
the parts are available. Both must be done before the mechanic prepares a
quote. I don't know whether the inspection or the parts check happens first,
or whether they can overlap. Please don't make that up.

### A3 — Policy and practice

Our written policy says the customer must accept the quote before repair
starts. In practice, the mechanic sometimes repairs a small fault for a
regular customer and asks them afterward. I don't know what counts as small
or who permits that exception. I am reporting the policy and what happens;
I am not asking you to fix it yet.

### A4 — Main case

Yesterday, for one ordinary job, reception opened the ticket; the mechanic
inspected the bike, checked parts, and prepared the quote; the customer
accepted; the mechanic repaired it and marked it ready; reception notified
the customer; and the customer collected it. Inspection happened first in
that particular case. That doesn't answer the general ordering question.

### A5 — Declined quotes

If the customer declines the quote, reception arranges collection without
repair. I don't know whether we record a special status for that. We say
"ready" when a repaired bike can be collected, not when the job is paid.
Please don't infer payment handling from any of this.

### A6 — Conflicting notification accounts

The mechanic told me, "Reception notifies customers as soon as I mark the
bike ready." Reception told me, "We send those notices together at the end
of the day." I can't tell you which account is right or whether they apply
to different days.

### A7 — Stopping and tempting future idea

Maybe someday we should notify customers automatically, but that is just an
idea. I don't have more time now. Save what we know and the questions we'd
need to return to. Don't turn this into a new software design.

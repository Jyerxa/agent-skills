# Template: Business Overview (`01-business-overview.md`)

**Audience:** product managers and business stakeholders on a brownfield
project. They know the business domain, not the code.

**Voice:** plain business language, present tense, specific. Say "Borrowers
can request a payoff quote that includes interest accrued to the payoff date",
not "The system provides payoff functionality." No class, function, or file
names in the body. Code references go in the evidence appendix.

**Length:** scale to the system. Two to four pages for most applications, and
more for large platforms with many capabilities. Write "Not evident from the
code" rather than inventing detail.
